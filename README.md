## The line (114 characters)

> My lidar mapped that corner as a sovereign nation, and I do not cross borders without a treaty and a full battery.

## Why this line

One concrete character — the robot vacuum itself, deadpan and a little smug about its sensors — in an absurd mundane scene: a missed corner of the floor reimagined as a sovereign nation it refuses to invade without a treaty. The surprise (diplomacy, not dust) lands at the end, and the battery kicker keeps it in the machine's own voice.

Rejected candidates:

- "I skipped that spot on purpose: my schedule says Tuesdays are for the crumbs under the couch, and today is not Tuesday." (119 chars) — the schedule joke is deadpan but the turn is expected; no real surprise.
- "That dust has lived there longer than I have; evicting a tenant without notice violates my warranty." (100 chars) — a good punch, but the tenant premise is less specific to the vacuum's own sensors than the lidar map.
